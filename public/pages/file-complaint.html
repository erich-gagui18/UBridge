    4Evidence

    Before you upload
    • Optional, but clear evidence helps SAEP assess your complaint faster.
    • Accepted: PNG, JPG or PDF, up to 5 files.
    • Use readable photos or screenshots. Show the date, place or people involved where you can.

      5Privacy & signature

      What happens to your information (summary)
      • What we keep: your account details, the complaint, your signature image, any evidence, and the case history.
      • Who sees it: you and authorized SAEP staff. The College Discipline Board may receive it for the hearing.
      • Where it runs: Google Firebase on the University’s behalf.
      • Your rights: access, correction, objection and erasure under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), subject to case-record rules.

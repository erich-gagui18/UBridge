About these terms

UBridge is the online complaint system of the Student Affairs and External Programs (SAEP) Office of the University of Batangas – Lipa. By signing in or creating an account you agree to these terms. They work together with the Privacy Notice, the College Student Discipline Policy and other University rules. They do not replace those rules, and they do not limit any right you have under the law.

Who may use UBridge

  • Students and faculty with a verified @ub.edu.ph account. SAEP Staff accounts are assigned by the SAEP Office only.
  • You are responsible for your account and password and for what is done with them. Tell the SAEP Office right away if you think someone else used your account.

What UBridge does

UBridge lets you file a complaint with the SAEP Office, follow it from your dashboard, and read information from the discipline policy through the AI Guide. Filing a complaint does not mean it is accepted. The SAEP Office assesses each complaint, and decisions are made by the people the policy names (the SAEP Office, the College Discipline Board, the department or the University President), never by the software.

What you agree to do

  • Give information that is true and complete as far as you know it.
  • Do not file a complaint to harass, retaliate against or embarrass someone, or file one you know is false. The policy may treat that as an offense.
  • Upload only evidence you have the right to share, and include only the personal information about other people that the complaint needs.
  • Keep information about a case confidential. Do not share what you can see in UBridge with people who are not part of the case.
  • Do not try to open other people’s records, get around the rules of the system, overload it, or upload files that can harm it.

The AI Guide

The AI Guide answers from the text of the College Student Discipline Policy. It can be wrong or incomplete. It is not legal advice and it is not an official ruling. The SAEP Office makes the final decision on every case. Do not type names or case details into the AI Guide.

SAEP staff

Staff may use case records and the email drafts only to handle complaints under the policy and the Data Privacy Act of 2012. UBridge does not send email. Staff check each draft and send it themselves from their own email account.

Availability

UBridge is a University service. It may be unavailable from time to time and its features may change. Keep your case number. If someone is in danger, contact campus security or local emergency services instead of using UBridge.

Misuse

The University may suspend an account that is used against these terms, and may handle the matter under its own rules.

Changes

If these terms change, the version number above changes. Using UBridge after a change means you accept the new version.

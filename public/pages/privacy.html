Privacy Notice

UBridge, SAEP Online Complaint Management System · University of Batangas – Lipa · Version 2026-10g

Draft for review. Items highlighted like this must be confirmed with the University’s Data Protection Officer (DPO) before real cases are filed. This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) but has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

UBridge is the online complaint system of the Student Affairs and External Programs (SAEP) Office of the University of Batangas – Lipa. SAEP is the personal information controller for the information described here. Complaints follow Form F-SAEP-07 and the UB College Student Discipline Policy (document SAEP M, Revision No. 4).

What we collect

  • Your account: name, UB email address, role (student, faculty or SAEP staff), and the profile details you save in My profile: contact number, student or employee number, department, course and year/section (students), and position. The complaint form copies these onto each complaint you file.
  • The complaint: the respondent’s name, student number, course, department and, if you give them, email and contact number; the offense type, date and place; and your description. This can include sensitive information about the respondent.
  • Your signature: the signature image you upload, shrunk to a small file, and your printed name.
  • Evidence: photos and PDFs you attach (up to five), shrunk or limited in size so they can be stored.
  • Case records: status history with remarks, hearing date, resolution note, who at SAEP made each change (visible to staff only), in-app notifications sent to you, and a staff-only record of each email SAEP staff say they sent about the case (to, subject, text, who and when).
  • On your device only: your theme, form drafts and recent AI Guide questions. They stay in your browser and are cleared when you sign out.

Why we collect it

To receive, assess, track and resolve student discipline complaints, to follow the College Student Discipline Policy, and to tell you when your case changes. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

Who can see it

  • You, for complaints you filed.
  • Authorized SAEP staff, who can see every complaint and its evidence.
  • The College Discipline Board and the respondent’s department, only as needed to handle the case under the College Student Discipline Policy and give the respondent a fair hearing: the policy requires that the respondent is notified in writing and receives a copy of the complaint with the statement of the complainant and any witnesses.
  • Others named in the policy: the respondent’s parent or guardian (always if the respondent is below 18, and for older students only with their consent), and, for the written decision, the Registrar’s Office, the VPSAS and the Office of the President. If a decision is appealed, the University President.
  • Google, which processes the data for the University through Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting). Its servers may be outside the Philippines. AI Guide questions go to Google’s Gemini service with the policy text needed to answer them, so do not type names or case details into the AI Guide.
  • Gmail (Google), only for SAEP staff who choose “Create Gmail draft”. The email text and the respondent’s email address are saved as a draft in that staff member’s own @ub.edu.ph Gmail account. UBridge never sends the email and does not keep Google’s access token.
  • The AI Guide search service, only if the University has switched it on. It finds the policy passages closest in meaning to your question, so your question and your sign-in token are sent to it. It is hosted outside Firebase, is built not to store or log questions, and never receives complaint records.

Other students and faculty cannot read your complaints.

Google user data

UBridge uses Google accounts and, optionally, Gmail. This is exactly what it asks for and why.

  • Sign in with Google (students, faculty and staff): Google gives UBridge your name and @ub.edu.ph email address. UBridge uses them only to create and recognize your UBridge account and to show your name on the complaints you file. It does not ask for access to your mailbox, contacts, Drive or calendar.
  • Gmail drafts (SAEP staff only, and only if the University switches the feature on): when a staff member presses “Create Gmail draft”, Google asks them to allow UBridge the gmail.compose permission. UBridge uses it for two things: to read the Gmail address of the signed-in account, so it can check that it ends in @ub.edu.ph, and to create a draft in that staff member’s own Gmail, or replace that draft if they edit it and save again. The draft holds the email text and the respondent’s address that the staff member chose. When the staff member confirms that the email was sent, UBridge also asks Gmail whether that one draft is still in the Drafts folder (a draft disappears when it is sent), only to warn about an email that was not sent yet. UBridge does not read, search or list any other email, does not read the content of any message in the mailbox, and does not send or delete anything.
  • What is kept: Google’s access token stays in the open page’s memory for about an hour and is not saved in the database or on the device. UBridge keeps your name and email address on your account, and, after a staff member confirms they sent an email, the record described under “What we collect”.
  • Limited Use: UBridge’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the sign-in and draft features above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and no one reads a staff member’s email through UBridge.
  • Taking access back: a staff member can remove UBridge’s Gmail permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we protect it

Only verified @ub.edu.ph accounts can sign in. Database rules let each person read only their own complaints, and only SAEP staff accounts can read all of them or change a case. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share your password.

How long we keep it

Retention period to be confirmed with the DPO and the SAEP Office, for example the period the Student Handbook or university records policy requires for discipline case files. When the period ends, SAEP will delete or anonymize the record.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, object to, or request erasure or blocking of your personal data, and you may complain to the National Privacy Commission. Because complaint records are kept for case handling, some requests may be limited where the law or university policy requires the record to be kept.

Consent

Before you submit a complaint, UBridge asks you to confirm that you read this notice and agree to the consent in Form F-SAEP-07: it lets the SAEP Office use your information solely for this complaint, stays valid for one (1) year, and can be revoked or cancelled in writing. The notice version you accepted is saved with the complaint. Uploading your signature shows that the complaint is yours.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that needs your consent again, the version number changes and you will be asked to accept it when you next file a complaint.

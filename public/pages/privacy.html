Privacy Notice

UBridge, SAEP Online Complaint Management System · University of Batangas – Lipa · Version 2026-10

Draft for review. Items highlighted like this must be confirmed with the University’s Data Protection Officer before the system is used for real cases. This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), but it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, UB email address and role (student, faculty or SAEP staff).
  • The complaint you file: the name, student ID, program and section of the student involved; the type, date and place of the incident; and your description. A complaint can contain sensitive personal information about the student named.
  • Case handling records: the status history of the case (who changed it, when, and any remark), hearing date, resolution note, and in-app notifications sent to you.
  • On your device only: theme, form drafts and recent AI Guide questions are kept in your browser and are not sent to us.

Why we collect it

To receive, assess, track and resolve student complaints handled by the SAEP Office, and to tell you when your case changes. We do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

You (for complaints you filed) and authorized SAEP staff. Other students and faculty cannot read your complaints. The system runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting), so Google processes the data on the University’s behalf. AI Guide questions are sent to Google’s Gemini service together with the handbook text needed to answer them; do not type names or case details into the AI Guide.

How long we keep it

Retention period to be confirmed with the DPO and the SAEP Office (for example, for as long as the case record is required by the Student Handbook or university policy).

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, object to, or request erasure or blocking of your personal data, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission. Because complaint records are kept for case handling, some requests may be limited where the law or university policy requires the record to be kept.

Consent

Before you submit a complaint, UBridge asks you to confirm that you read this notice and consent to the processing described here. The version you accepted is saved with the complaint.

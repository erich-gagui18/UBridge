How do I file a complaint?

Sign in, open File a complaint, complete the steps, accept the privacy notice and submit. You get a case number such as SAEP-2026-001 to track it.

What do the statuses mean?

Submitted: SAEP has received it. In review: SAEP is assessing the degree of the offense and routing the complaint. For hearing: it moved to a hearing. Resolved: the case is closed.

Can I edit a complaint after submitting?

Not from your account. Contact the SAEP Office if something needs correcting.

Who can see my complaint?

Only you and SAEP staff. See the Privacy Notice.

What evidence can I attach?

Evidence uploads are not available yet, so no files are stored. Describe the evidence in your complaint and bring the originals to the SAEP Office with your case number. When uploads are switched on, you can attach PNG, JPG or PDF files, up to 10 MB each and 5 files.

Is the AI Guide an official ruling?

No. It answers only from the UB College Student Discipline Policy (SAEP M, Revision No. 4), shows the part it used, and says so when it can’t find an answer. SAEP makes the final decision.

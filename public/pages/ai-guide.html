How do I file a complaint?

Sign in, open File a complaint, complete the three steps and submit. You get a case number such as SAEP-2026-001 to track it.

What do the statuses mean?

Submitted: SAEP has received it. In review: staff are assessing it. For hearing: it moved to a hearing. Resolved: the case is closed.

Can I edit a complaint after submitting?

Not from your account. Contact the SAEP Office if something needs correcting.

Who can see my complaint?

Only you and SAEP staff.

What evidence can I attach?

PNG, JPG or PDF, up to 10 MB each and 5 files. If uploads are switched off in this deployment, only the file names are saved.

Is the AI Guide an official ruling?

No. It answers from the handbook sections it has and shows the section it used. SAEP makes the final decision.

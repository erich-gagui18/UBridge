UBridge, the SAEP Online Complaint Management System

UBridge is the online complaint system of the SAEP Office of the University of Batangas – Lipa. Students and faculty file a discipline complaint on Form F-SAEP-07, follow it from submission to resolution, and ask an AI Guide what the College Student Discipline Policy says.

Sign in or create an account Read the Privacy Notice

Open to verified @ub.edu.ph accounts. SAEP Staff accounts are assigned by the SAEP Office.

What UBridge does

File a complaint

Fill in the SAEP complaint form online: the respondent, the offense chosen from the policy’s First, Second and Third Degree list, what happened, optional evidence, and your signature. You receive a case number such as SAEP-2026-001.

Track every stage

Follow your case through Submitted, In review, For hearing and Resolved, with a dated history and in-app notifications when SAEP updates it.

Ask the AI Guide

Ask about offense degrees, sanctions or how a complaint is handled. Answers come only from the College Student Discipline Policy and show the section they used. The guide is not an official ruling.

For SAEP staff

Review every case, update its status and handling steps, and prepare emails to respondents from templates. Staff check each email and send it themselves.

How UBridge uses your Google account

UBridge asks for the least Google access it needs, and only for the reasons below. The full wording is in the Privacy Notice.

Sign in with Google (everyone)

You can sign in with your @ub.edu.ph Google account instead of a password. UBridge uses your name and UB email address to create and recognize your UBridge account. It does not access your mailbox, contacts, Drive or calendar.

Gmail drafts (SAEP staff, optional)

If the University turns this on, a staff member can press Create Gmail draft. UBridge then asks permission to create email drafts in that staff member’s own @ub.edu.ph Gmail (the gmail.compose permission). The staff member opens the draft in Gmail, checks it and sends it. UBridge does not read, search, list, send or delete any email, and it keeps no Google access token after the page closes.

UBridge’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.

Who it is for and where to get help

  • Students and faculty of the University of Batangas – Lipa with a verified @ub.edu.ph email address can file and track complaints.
  • SAEP Office staff handle complaints under the College Student Discipline Policy. Staff accounts are assigned by the office, never self-registered.
  • Questions: email the SAEP Office (Lipa City) at saep.ublc@ub.edu.ph. If someone is in danger, contact campus security or local emergency services instead of using UBridge.
